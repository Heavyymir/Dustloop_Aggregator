Story

Hyde is our high schooler protagonist. The power of EXS awakened within Hyde after an encounter with a mysterious young girl named Linne. After a series of gruesome battles, Hyde defeated Paradox--considered to be the strongest wielder of all.

Afterwards, however, Hyde was defeated by an immensely powerful Re-Birth called Kuon the Aeon. Kuon boasted of plans that involved destroying the world as a means to achieve his goal. Hearing this, Linne disappeared. Hyde sets out on his own to confront Kuon and save the world...

Gameplay

As the protagonist, Hyde has the usual "shoto" tools associated with the role (fireball, meterless reversal, etc). He performs well at all ranges, able to zone and cover his approach with moves like Black Orbiter and Blaring Outrage and counter zoners and long range attacks with Shadow Scare (and Blaring Outrage), while moves like 66C help him close the distance and start running pressure. The excellent corner carry Vacant Shift provides makes it easy for Hyde to push his opponent to the corner, which is where he truly excels, while also still dealing excellent damage anywhere. His fast normals and varied ways to make himself safe or plus on block, either through rebeats or spending meter, make him difficult to contest and set up a strong and very rewarding strike/throw game. Additionally, his sword normals do chip damage, meaning that even if the opponent is content to block, damage can still add up quickly.


Playstyle
Hyde is an all-rounder who excels most anywhere, but especially in close-quarters thanks to his strong pressure and lockdown capability.
Strengths Weaknesses
  • Solid Neutral: Hyde has strong spatial coverage, good mid-range pokes, two fireballs, and well-rounded movement speed.
  • High damage: Hyde consistently achieves above-average damage, with even higher damage in the corner. His corner meter dumps are arguably the best in the game. Additionally, his throw gives a combo with either a wallbounce in the corner or with CVO.
  • Oppressive Offense: Hyde shines at locking down his opponent through the use of high-reward frametraps, good rebeat frame data, and easily-accessible plus frames. He is also good at using spacing to run RPS with a backdash that's tied for fastest in the game, and shielding him can be very risky due to his high reward off his callouts for it.
  • Strong Defense: Hyde has several reversals (623B, Veil Bringer, Gyro Vortex), a notably great backdash, solid abare options, good throw OSes (191AD, 3CAD), and a low crush (214X).
  • Chip Damage: All of Hyde's sword normals deal chip damage, with his Vorpal Trait further increasing the chip damage of certain moves.
  • Bleeds GRD: Hyde's plus-on-block string enders (236A, 22B, 2B+C), which are needed to make bigger gaps in his pressure, are easy to shield or inherently cost GRD. While Hyde is by no means forced to rely on his Force Functions, they are very strong tools but can easily cost him the Vorpal Cycle if he isn't careful. However, Hyde can somewhat compensate for this with the additional GRD gain from his corner throw combo and his ability to stall for cycle using his 214C combo ender.
  • Midscreen Throw: If the opponent immediately backtechs after taking a midscreen throw, meatying becomes stricter requiring you to time 66C or dash 3B.


Unique Mechanics: Chip Damage

All of Hyde's sword normals deal chip damage. It isn't much in the grand scheme, but it adds up fast when Hyde is able to keep the opponent locked down.

Hyde also deals extra chip damage on certain specials:
